# Standard acceptance models (v1)

Generated by `AcceptanceModel` (`test-grid/.../AcceptanceModel.kt`, generator version 1) and bundled in
`test-grid/src/main/resources/testgrid/models/` with `models.json`, which publishes each file's SHA-256. Test Mode and
the CLI refuse a model file whose hash differs (`ModelLibrary.materialize`). `AcceptanceModelTest` regenerates the files
and requires them to be byte-identical to the bundled ones, the mesh to be closed and 2-manifold with outward normals,
and the published dimensions to match the geometry.

```bash
./gradlew -q :test-grid:cli --args="models --check"          # regenerate and compare
./gradlew -q :test-grid:cli --args="models --write <dir>"    # write fresh copies
```

The models are axis-aligned boxes meshed on the grid their faces define (binary STL, little-endian, fixed header),
so the same generator version always writes the same bytes. They are small enough to review and cheap to print.

## `nozzle-acceptance-v1` (single material)

60 × 60 mm footprint, 0.6 mm base plate. SHA-256 `f91f4bad851566cdf80d12d46bfeca8c042e3998af849c76323497c2cf052439`.

| Feature | Geometry (mm) | Tests | Acceptance in the reference suites |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base plate | 60 × 60 × 0.6 | bed placement, first layer, adhesion | first layer judged pass/partial/fail with a photo; model centred within 5 mm (G-code check) and printed "centre" (operator) |
| Hollow tower | 20 × 20 outside, 8 × 8 through-hole, 10.6 tall overall | X/Y/Z accuracy, surface | outside 20.0 ± 0.25, hole 8.0 ± 0.3, height 10.6 ± 0.25; walls judged |
| Two pillars | 4 × 4, 15.6 tall, 12 apart | retraction, stringing | stringing judged |
| Bridge | 1.2 deck on two 4 mm piers, 18 unsupported span, 9.8 tall, 20 wide | bridging | underside judged with a photo |
| Thin wall | 1.2 × 20 × 8.6 | extrusion width | 1.2 ± 0.2 |

Bed limits are checked in software: every extruding move of the sliced file must lie inside the profile's printable
area (`within_bed`), and the model must be centred (`centered`, which ignores start-G-code purge lines).

## `nozzle-acceptance-mm-v1` (multi-material; never part of single-material acceptance)

Two parts, one per tool or lane: four 10 × 40 × 3 mm stripes alternating between part A (tool 1) and part B (tool 2),
40 × 40 mm overall. Tests tool or material changes, purge and colour bleed at boundaries. SHA-256: part A
`c1338f179a8b254ce6deba0d3df288de8e5c65fe1f453f35e1e848210470b8b9`, part B
`324aa51655b05ede6a1cb7872ce93ad63ae987bcb686f706cc88925284760946`.

Confirmed on a device: the on-device engine keeps the two parts' own coordinates when they are sliced as one project
(`SlicingCoordinator.sliceProject`); the owner's PAXX U1 printed the two-tool part as alternating stripes (bundles
`58f24794` and `2d3afb85`, [evidence/README.md](evidence/README.md)).

## `nozzle-colour-swatch-v1` (colour mixing; multi-material suites only)

One 40 × 10 × 6 mm block, about 30 layers at 0.2 mm. The `mix-slice` test assigns it to a 50/50 mix of tools 1 and 2
through the printer's own mixing system: Full Spectrum on a Snapmaker U1, PrusaSlicer ColorMix on other printers with
two or more slots, the same "+ Add 50/50" the project editor offers. The sliced file must alternate its two filaments
layer by layer (`alternates_tools`). The printed swatch should read as one blended colour at arm's length. SHA-256
`4e56f2cf29166733f15fe4170c850c26574af0de4a7378eef5822e6ce53f4e40`. Not yet run on a device.

The 20 mm test cube (`site-src/assets/test-cube-20mm.stl`) remains the quick smoke-test part; it is not a Test Grid
acceptance model. The owner's clean U1 print of that cube on 2026-09-28 is prior history, not Test Grid evidence.
