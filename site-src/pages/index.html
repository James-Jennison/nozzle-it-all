Slice it. Send it. Nail it.

Slice 3D models on your phone. Control your printers from anywhere.

Nozzle It All is a free, open-source Android app with an on-device OrcaSlicer engine and remote printer control over Wi-Fi or your phone's mobile data. Use a private connection you choose; your printer traffic never passes through a Nozzle It All server. No account. No analytics.

Android {{status:android}} In invite-only closed testing on Google Play. Not yet publicly available.

What it does

Slice on your phone

An on-device engine built from OrcaSlicer turns STL, 3MF and OBJ files into G-code, with a 3D plate, support painting and a layer-by-layer preview. Nothing is uploaded to a cloud slicer.

Control from anywhere

At home over Wi-Fi or away over mobile data, monitor temperatures and progress, start, pause and cancel prints, browse files and use the camera. Connect through Tailscale (recommended), Cloudflare Tunnel, OctoEverywhere or another private route you manage. Set up remote access.

Find something to print

Browse free models from MyMiniFactory inside the app. The designer's credit is saved with every project you start from a model.

Alerts when it matters

Optional background notifications when a print finishes, fails or a printer goes offline. Off until you turn them on.

Works with

Some printer types are confirmed on real hardware and others are built from the vendors' published protocols. The printers page and the app say which is which, and we do not claim more than we have tested.

Verified on hardware Klipper / Moonraker, Snapmaker U1
Built from vendor docs Bambu Lab (LAN mode), OctoPrint, PrusaLink

Your printers stay yours

No account. No analytics. No ads. The Android app talks to your printers directly over your own network, and to MyMiniFactory when you browse models. It sends nothing to us, and there is no Nozzle It All account or cloud service behind it. The planned web app is designed the same way: its slicing and printer connections go to an engine you run, not to us. Read the privacy policy.

One app today, more platforms planned

Android comes first. A Linux desktop app, a web app and a Windows desktop app are planned, in that order. iOS and macOS are deferred. None of the planned ones exist yet.
