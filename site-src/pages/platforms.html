Platforms

Nozzle It All is one product family: the same projects, printers, words and safety rules on each platform, with an interface suited to each. Android is the version you can try today, in invite-only closed testing. Desktop and Web are built and being tested, and aren't available yet. Nothing below links to a download until it's real.

{{platform_cards}}

Android {{status:android}}

The full app for your phone: on-device slicing, printer control at home over Wi-Fi or away over mobile data through a private connection you choose, model discovery and background alerts. Nozzle It All connects directly to the printer address you configure and doesn't relay printer traffic through its own server. It's in invite-only closed testing on Google Play. To hear when it's public, follow the releases on GitHub.

Want to try it early? Email us to request a spot in the closed test and include the Google account address you use on Google Play. How the test works.

Desktop {{status:linux}}

Built around the printers you own: a home screen showing your whole fleet, projects, a guided Prepare workflow, printing and monitoring with cameras, materials and toolheads, and Full Spectrum. For specialist work it includes the Advanced Workspace, a full-featured editor built from OrcaSlicer, which opens as a separate window you enter on purpose. Your project comes back to Nozzle when you close it. Linux comes first. It's built and being tested and isn't available to download yet.

Web {{status:web}}

The whole app in your browser, slicing on your own device. It isn't a hosted cloud slicer. How Nozzle It All Web works.

Windows {{status:windows}}

Planned after Linux, packaged from the same Desktop app.

iOS and macOS {{status:ios}}

Deferred, which means paused rather than cancelled. There's no plan or timeline to announce.

Moving between them

Desktop and Web save projects as standard 3MF files with Nozzle's details inside, so a project prepared in one opens in the other with its printer, materials and settings intact, and other 3MF slicers can still open it. Android currently uses its own project file; moving it to the same 3MF format is planned.

Order and status are the current intention and can change. This page is updated when something is actually released.
