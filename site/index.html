Slice it. Send it. Nail it.

Prepare, slice and control your 3D printers. On your own devices.

Nozzle It All is a free, open-source family of apps for your phone, your computer and your browser. Each one slices on the device in front of you and talks to your printers directly, at home or through a private connection you choose. Your printer traffic never passes through a Nozzle It All server. No account. No analytics.

What it does

Slice on your phone

An on-device engine built from OrcaSlicer turns STL, 3MF and OBJ files into G-code, with a 3D plate, support painting and a layer-by-layer preview. Nothing is uploaded to a cloud slicer.

Control from anywhere

At home over Wi-Fi or away over mobile data, monitor temperatures and progress, start, pause and cancel prints, browse files and use the camera. Connect through Tailscale (recommended), Cloudflare Tunnel, OctoEverywhere or another private route you manage. Set up remote access.

Find something to print

Browse free models from MyMiniFactory inside the app. The designer's credit is saved with every project you start from a model.

Alerts when it matters

Optional background notifications when a print finishes, fails or a printer goes offline. Off until you turn them on.

Works with

Made first for the Snapmaker U1 with PAXX firmware. PAXX works entirely on your own network: no Snapmaker account, no cloud and no internet connection needed. Stock-firmware U1 printers are supported separately, and that support never gets in the way of PAXX.

Some printer types are confirmed on real hardware and others are built from the vendors' published protocols. The printers page and the app say which is which, and we do not claim more than we have tested.

Verified on hardware Klipper / Moonraker, Snapmaker U1 with PAXX firmware
Built from vendor docs Bambu Lab (LAN mode), OctoPrint, PrusaLink

Your printers stay yours

No account. No analytics. No ads. The Android app talks to your printers directly over your own network, and to MyMiniFactory when you browse models. It sends nothing to us, and there is no Nozzle It All account or cloud service behind it. Nozzle It All for Desktop and Nozzle It All Web, both in development, are built the same way: they slice on your device and talk to your printers directly. Read the privacy policy.

One family: Android, Desktop and Web
